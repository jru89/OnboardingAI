# Mock Scenario: Write a Project Update

**How to use this file:** Download it, then paste its contents (or upload the file itself) into your Claude Code session with a request like: *"Turn these status bullet points into a short written update I can send to a stakeholder. Keep it to a few short paragraphs, and lead with the headline."*

This one is good practice for taking rough, fragmented notes and turning them into something a busy person could read in under a minute.

---

## Raw Status Bullets: "Wayfinding Signs — Phase 2" (Riverbend Community Library project)

Loose notes taken by Sam Okafor (Project Lead, Acme Fictional Co.) ahead of a stakeholder check-in:

- Overall: project is behind schedule but not at risk of falling through — confidence is still high it lands well.
- Design work: 100% done and approved by the client back in June. No open questions on design.
- Fabrication: signage panels are cut and printed, sitting at the vendor's shop.
- Blocker: powder-coating step is delayed — vendor's coating line had a backlog from a larger unrelated job. Not our error, but it's the thing holding everything up.
- Revised estimate: last week of next month for coated panels to ship, but that date is "vendor's best guess," not firm yet — expect to confirm in about a week.
- Budget: on track, no overage expected even with the delay (fabrication cost was fixed in the contract).
- Install: once panels arrive, on-site installation is expected to take 2 business days. Library has already agreed to close the affected wing for those 2 days once a date is set.
- Risk to flag: if the vendor slips again, install could bump into the library's annual fundraising gala (mid-following-month) — would want to avoid disruption during that event.
- Team morale note (probably skip in the external update): the delay has been frustrating for the fabrication sub-contractor too, they've been proactive about communicating.

## Things to try in your Claude Code session

- Ask Claude for a short update aimed at a client stakeholder (Riverbend's librarian) — notice what it chooses to include or leave out, like the internal "team morale" note that probably shouldn't go to a client.
- Ask for a second version aimed at an internal stakeholder (your own manager at Acme) and compare what changes in tone and level of detail.
- Try asking Claude to lead with the headline (behind schedule, but on budget and design is done) rather than burying it — a good habit for status updates in general.
